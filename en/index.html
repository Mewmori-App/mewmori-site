It gets to know you

"I want to know you better! 😸" — that's how the first day starts. The cat asks your name and what you do, and you get to pick a name for it.

After that it remembers: what you're working on, what you like, what you keep coming back to. It knows your mornings and evenings, notices habits — which app you open at this hour — and builds little rituals around them.

The bond goes through six stages — from wary stranger to family. The cat behaves differently at each: at first it keeps to the edge of the screen and speaks briefly, later it calls you by name, and in the end it waits for you at login and brings up things you did together.

Memory that never reaches the cloud

Everything the cat knows about you stays on your computer. The language model runs locally too — there is nowhere to send your messages, because Mewmori has no server.

Memory is split by source: conversations and screen are switched on separately. Don't want it remembering? Turn it off — the cat stays the same.

The model is 1.4 GB and downloads only when you first want to talk. Without it the cat is still fully alive.

You can play with it

Games right on the desktop — plus achievements that unlock on their own.

🙈 Hide-and-seek

The cat vanishes to a random spot on screen and turns almost transparent. Find it and poke it before the time runs out.

🖱️ Chase

It hunts your cursor: stalks in an arc, freezes, aims and leaps ahead of where you're going. The leap can't be corrected — so you can still dodge at the last moment.

⭕ Tic-tac-toe

It doesn't play by rules baked into the code — the moves come from that same 2B model, running on your machine with no internet.

🌿 Garden expeditions

The cat walks off to the garden gate, heads out on an expedition and comes back with loot.

There are achievements too: "First click", "Night owl" for opening the menu after midnight, "You fell for it!" for that fake error, and "Loyal friend" for the tenth launch.

It needs looking after

The cat gets hungry, its mood shifts, and it has a goal for the day. Feed it its favourite fish and it'll be far happier than with a plain snack.

Personality and chattiness are adjustable — from a quiet flatmate to a cat that has an opinion about everything. So is its size, from tiny to large.

Two cats come with the app. The rest — the fold, siamese, calico, munchkin and sphynx — unlock with a single purchase, forever, along with crowns, hats and wings.

Pricing and how to buy

Questions

Mostly about privacy and about that 1.4 GB.

Does it really see my screen?

It looks at what's happening in the active window and decides how to behave and what to say. All of it happens on your computer: screenshots, window text and file names are never transmitted. Details are in the privacy policy.

What exactly does it remember?

Facts about you, preferences, projects and plans — the things that let it pick up a conversation a week later. Memory is stored locally and split by source: conversations and screen can be toggled separately, and anything remembered can be reviewed and deleted.

Why download 1.4 GB?

That's the language model for conversations, and it runs on your computer rather than in the cloud — hence the size. It downloads only with your consent, and only when you first want to talk; you can tap "later" and live with the cat without it.

Will it get in the way of work?

Chattiness is adjustable, and if you need complete silence, send the cat to sleep in the menu bar from its menu. It comes back when you call.

Will it drain my battery?

Most of the time the cat is just animating and computing next to nothing. The load appears while the model answers you — and ends with the answer.

What does it cost?

The app is free, and so are two of the cats and all the games. Only extra skins and accessories are paid, from 60 ⭐ in Telegram Stars, bought once and working on all your Macs. Full list is on the pricing page.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up at all: a purchase is tied to a licence key that can be restored by the email address used at checkout.
