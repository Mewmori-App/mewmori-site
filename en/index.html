The usual questions

Questions

Does it really see my screen?

It looks at what's happening in the active window and decides how to behave and what to say. Screenshots, window text, and file names are never transmitted.

What exactly does it remember?

Facts about you, preferences, projects, and plans: the things that let it pick up a conversation a week later. Memory is stored locally; sources can be switched off, and saved entries can be reviewed and deleted.

Why download 1.4 GB?

That's the language model for conversations, and it runs on your computer. It downloads only when you first want to talk; without it the cat is still alive.

Will it get in the way of work?

Chattiness is adjustable. When you need complete silence, send the cat to sleep in the menu bar. It comes back when you call.

What does it cost?

The app, two cats, and the games are free. Extra looks and accessories are bought once, with no subscription. Full list on the pricing page.
