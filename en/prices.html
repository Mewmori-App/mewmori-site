Pricing

Updated 4 August 2026

The Mewmori app itself is free. What you pay for are extra digital items: cat skins, accessories and bundles. Prices are in Telegram Stars: outside Russia you buy in the @mewmori_shop_bot bot, and Telegram requires digital goods to be sold in Stars. A purchase is perpetual and works on every computer you own; there is no subscription and no recurring charge.

How to buy

1. Open the bot

Send /start to @mewmori_shop_bot and pick a skin, accessory or bundle from the list.

2. Pay with Stars

Telegram charges the Stars from your balance. There is no card step and no payment details for us to see.

3. Get your key

The bot sends your licence key straight into the chat. Paste it into Mewmori → "Wardrobe" and everything unlocks.

The key is cumulative: it carries all of your purchases at once and works on any number of your Macs. Lost it? Send /restore to the bot and it reissues the key for your Telegram account. If you paid by card instead, restore it by email.

More on payment methods and delivery times is on the payment and delivery page; the terms of purchase are in the public offer, and refunds are covered on the refunds page.
