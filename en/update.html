Your updates are switched off, and that is our fault

12 September 2026

Versions 1.0.14 through 1.0.21 asked, on first launch, for permission to check for updates. Close that window and the app never checks again. We fixed it in 1.0.22, but the fix rides inside an update, and the update cannot reach you. We have no way in from our side, hence this note.

Do it by hand, once: click the cat in the menu bar at the top of your screen, then "Check for updates".

What you have missed since August

  • the cat keeps quiet while you type or take a call, and there is a quiet hour now
  • a wardrobe with seasonal sets
  • stroke him with the cursor and he purrs
  • he falls asleep when you step away, and follows you to another monitor
  • a couple of crash fixes

Sorry about that.

If the check does not work for some reason, install the current version over the old one: download Mewmori. Your settings and your cat stay where they are.
